SERVICES

Everything you need to know before ordering a website.

GENERAL

Worldwide Services

Noir Digital provides website services internationally, serving clients from any country, region, or continent.

Websites for Any Purpose

We create websites for a wide range of purposes, from business websites and portfolios to landing pages and multipurpose websites.

Fully Customized

When ordering a website, you can fully customize your project. Share your specific ideas, features, designs, or concepts, and we will work to bring them to life as closely as possible.

Changes Before Delivery

If you change your mind about an idea or design while your website is still being developed, you can send a follow-up email explaining what you would like changed.

After Delivery

Noir Digital does not offer paid monthly maintenance. Websites are delivered as a one-time purchase.

Once the website has been delivered and confirmed as functional, any future changes or fixes to the website will be charged separately from the original website price.

PRICES & DELIVERY

Dynamic Pricing

Website prices are dynamic and depend on the size, design, level of detail, functionality, and purpose of the website.

Quote Time

The estimated time to receive a price quote is 1–5 days, depending primarily on the size and complexity of the website and the current order queue.

Price Range

€20 — €400

The final price may vary depending on the requirements of the individual project.

Waiting Time

Noir Digital will make every effort to complete and deliver each website as soon as possible.

The expected delivery time is within 14 days, depending on the size and complexity of the website, the amount of customization required, and the current order queue.

Customers will be informed if a project requires additional time.
